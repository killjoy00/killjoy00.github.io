Privacy Policy

Ultimate Tic/Tac/Toe is playable without creating a separate account. Most game information stays on your device. On iPhone, Apple Game Center and StoreKit handle online matches and purchases. On Android, Google Play Billing handles purchases where available. Google advertising services may process information when banner advertising is enabled.

1. Games and information saved on your device

Offline matches, game moves and replays, scores, match statistics, wins and streaks, appearance and sound settings, and the choice to skip or finish the tutorial are stored on the iPhone. We do not operate a separate game-account system, game analytics service, or developer-controlled server that receives your saved local match history.

Deleting the app removes local app data, subject to normal device backups. Game Center and App Store purchase information may remain with Apple; Google Play purchase records may remain with Google.

2. Apple Game Center (iPhone)

On iPhone, if you use online matchmaking, turn-based matches, achievements or leaderboards, Apple's Game Center handles the associated player identity, gaming activity, invitations, matches, outcomes and leaderboard entries. Game Center may send notifications when it is your turn. Your Apple Account settings and Apple's privacy practices govern this information.

Android currently offers local and computer play without online multiplayer. We do not run an independent online matchmaking or push-notification server. To manage Game Center on iPhone, open Game Center settings. See Apple's privacy information.

3. Google advertising and consent

When advertising is enabled, the free version uses Google AdMob banner advertisements and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for regional privacy/consent choices. Depending on your location, consent decisions, device settings and Google's advertising configuration, these services may process:

  • Device or app identifiers (which may include an advertising identifier where applicable).
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Advertisements shown and advertising interactions, app interactions and usage data.
  • Crash reports, performance information and other diagnostic data.

Google may use this information to deliver advertisements, measure their performance, reduce fraud, improve its services and provide analytics. The app checks UMP's consent/eligibility status before requesting banner ads. Whether a consent form appears depends on regional requirements and the state of consent.

When available in the installed app version, a verified one-time Remove Ads purchase stops banner ad requests. Advertising and consent information already held by Google is subject to Google's own retention and privacy controls. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Business Data Responsibility.

4. Optional in-app purchase

The optional Remove Ads purchase uses Apple's StoreKit on iPhone, or Google Play Billing on Android where available. Apple or Google processes payments and manages purchase records for their respective platforms. The app checks the store-provided purchase entitlement to decide whether it should display banners. We do not receive payment-card information. Purchases, refunds and revocations are managed by the applicable app store.

5. Your privacy choices

  • Advertising consent: Where Google's consent system requires a privacy-options control, open the game's More → Remove ads screen on iPhone, or Settings on Android when available, and choose Advertising privacy choices.
  • Apple privacy and tracking settings: Review device privacy settings; on iPhone this includes Settings → Privacy & Security and Game Center.
  • Remove local game information: Delete the app to remove locally stored game data, subject to device backups.
  • Apple and Google information: Use their respective privacy settings and support processes to manage data they independently hold.

For a shorter walkthrough, see our Privacy Choices page.

6. Retention and disclosure

Locally saved game information remains on your device until removed through deletion or device-data management. Apple and Google manage their own data storage, retention and deletion under their respective policies. We do not sell a separate database of player match histories, and we do not share those local histories from a developer-operated backend.

7. General audience

Ultimate Tic/Tac/Toe is a general-audience board game, not a service designed specifically for children. Apple Game Center eligibility and parental controls, as well as applicable Google advertising rules, still apply.

8. This website

These information pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Visiting the website may result in GitHub processing ordinary technical web-request information according to its own privacy practices. The game does not use the website to receive your saved local matches.

9. Questions and updates

For support or a privacy question, visit our Support page or open a GitHub issue. GitHub issues are public, so do not include contact details, transaction receipts, account identifiers or other sensitive information in a public post. For information held directly by Apple or Google, use the privacy channels linked above.

We may update this page if the game or its third-party services change. The date at the top identifies the most recent revision.
