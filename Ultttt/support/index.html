Here's how to get playing, fix common issues and send feedback about Ultimate Tic/Tac/Toe.

Updated November 17, 2026

Getting started

On a new installation, the optional tutorial explains how the nine connected boards work. Choose Show me how to practice or Skip tutorial · Play now to start immediately. You can replay the walkthrough any time from More → Interactive tutorial.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play online?

Tap Play online in the game. You'll need to be signed in to Apple Game Center. Use Apple's game invitations or matchmaking to find an opponent. Game Center manages online turns and may notify you when it's your turn.

How do I switch between light and dark mode?

Open More → Settings and select Follow iPhone, Light or Dark. Sound effects and haptics are also configurable there.

Why can't I play in a particular small board?

Every square you choose sends the next player to the corresponding small board. The outlined board is the one you must use. If that destination board has already been won or filled, the next player may choose any unfinished small board.

How do I remove ads or restore a purchase?

Open More → Remove ads. Remove Ads is a one-time, non-consumable purchase (U.S. price: $1.99). If you've purchased it before, tap Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. The app enables ad-free play only after StoreKit verifies the purchase.

Where are my game records stored?

Offline games, saved progress, replays and statistics are stored locally on your iPhone. They don't sync through a separate developer account. Online matches, achievements and leaderboards are managed by Game Center.

I don't see online turns or notifications.

Check that Game Center is signed in on your iPhone and that the relevant notification settings allow Game Center alerts. Open the game to refresh your turn count. Online matches require a network connection and two participating Game Center accounts.

Report a bug or suggest an improvement

You can open a support issue on GitHub. Include your iOS version, app build number, what you expected, and what happened. If possible, describe how to reproduce it. GitHub requires a login to create an issue.

Keep reports private-safe. GitHub issues are public. Do not post your Apple Account details, passwords, receipts, device IDs, or other sensitive information.

Privacy and purchases

Read our Privacy Policy and Privacy Choices. App Store purchases and refunds are handled by Apple; for refund requests visit Apple's Report a Problem.
