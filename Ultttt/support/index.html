Support

Here's how to get playing, fix common issues and send feedback about Ultimate Tic/Tac/Toe.

Updated November 19, 2026

Getting started

On a new installation, the optional tutorial explains how the nine connected boards work. You can skip it and start immediately. On iPhone, revisit it through More → Interactive tutorial. On Android, use How to play.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play online?

Online games are currently available on iPhone via Play online and Apple Game Center. Android currently supports computer and local two-player play. On iPhone, you'll need to be signed in to Game Center. Use Apple's game invitations or matchmaking to find an opponent. Game Center manages online turns and may notify you when it's your turn.

How do I switch between light and dark mode?

Open Settings on Android or More → Settings on iPhone to select your appearance. iPhone also offers sound effects and haptics.

Why can't I play in a particular small board?

Every square you choose sends the next player to the corresponding small board. The outlined board is the one you must use. If that destination board has already been won or filled, the next player may choose any unfinished small board.

How do I remove ads or restore a purchase?

On iPhone, open More → Remove ads for the one-time non-consumable purchase (U.S. price: $1.99). On Android, the Remove Ads option appears in Settings only after its Google Play Billing product is configured and available. Use Restore purchases with the app-store account that purchased it. Banners are disabled only after the applicable store confirms ownership.

Where are my game records stored?

Offline games, saved progress, replays and statistics are stored locally on your device. They don't sync through a separate developer account. Online matches, achievements and leaderboards on iPhone are managed by Game Center.

I don't see online turns or notifications.

On iPhone, check that Game Center is signed in and that the relevant notification settings allow Game Center alerts. Open the game to refresh your turn count. Online matches require a network connection and two participating Game Center accounts.

Report a bug or suggest an improvement

You can open a support issue on GitHub. Include your Android/iOS version, app build number, what you expected, and what happened. If possible, describe how to reproduce it. GitHub requires a login to create an issue.

Keep reports private-safe. GitHub issues are public. Do not post your Apple or Google Account details, passwords, receipts, device IDs, or other sensitive information.

Privacy and purchases

Read our Privacy Policy and Privacy Choices. App Store purchases and refunds are handled by Apple; Google Play purchases and refunds are handled by Google. For iPhone refunds, visit Apple's Report a Problem.
