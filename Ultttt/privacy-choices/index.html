Privacy Choices

You can play offline without a separate game account, manage advertising choices where available, and control your Apple Game Center activity.

Updated November 17, 2026

Manage advertising choices

  1. Open Ultimate Tic/Tac/Toe on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the More menu (three dots), then Remove ads.
  3. Where required by Google's consent system, tap Advertising privacy choices and follow the displayed instructions. The option only appears when the SDK says a privacy-options form is required.

Not seeing that option? Consent controls vary by region and SDK status. You can also review Google My Ad Center and your iPhone privacy settings.

Play without banner advertisements

The app offers a one-time Remove Ads purchase through Apple's App Store. When a purchase is verified, the app stops requesting banners. To restore a prior purchase, open More → Remove ads → Restore purchases. Apple handles payment, refunds and purchase history.

Control your Game Center activity

Open your iPhone's Settings → Game Center to manage your Apple gaming account and available privacy settings. Game Center matches and leaderboards are handled by Apple, not by a developer-hosted matchmaking server.

Delete saved game progress

Scores, local moves, replays, statistics and tutorial preferences reside on the device. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to iOS device backups. It does not automatically erase Game Center or Apple purchase records.

Other questions?

Read our full Privacy Policy or visit Support. Do not share personal or payment details in public GitHub issues.
